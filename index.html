Business intelligence definitions

Total revenue collected
Sum of every payment received in the payment ledger, across current and earlier membership terms.
Pending fees
Total membership fee minus amount paid, summed across current memberships. Never below zero.
Active subscription
Start date is on or before today and end date is on or after today.
Expiring soon
An active subscription whose end date is within the next 7 days.
Expired subscription
End date is before today.
New trainees this month
Trainees whose registration (join) date falls in the current calendar month.
Renewal rate
Renewed trainees divided by eligible trainees. Eligible means a trainee who has reached the end of a term: those who renewed at least once, plus those whose membership expired without renewal.
Revenue growth
(Current period revenue minus previous period revenue) divided by previous period revenue, times 100. Shown only when the previous period has revenue. Insights compare month to date with the same number of days last month.
Data completeness
Correctly populated required fields divided by total required fields, times 100.
Data accuracy score
Average of five measures: field completeness, valid subscription dates, correct pending-fee calculation, unique trainee IDs and valid fee/payment records.
Overpayments
Not allowed. No advance-payment rule is implemented, so Amount paid can never exceed the membership fee.

About this project

SMASH GYM is a frontend-only Business Intelligence project built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript and Chart.js. Records are stored in this browser's localStorage. The admin login is for academic demonstration and is not production security.
